WEST COAST CLEANERS — FACEBOOK AD ENGINE
Help guide for clients
==========================================

This tool creates ready-to-post Facebook group ads for West Coast Cleaners:
short copy (headline, body, call-to-action, hashtags) plus a square image you can
download and upload to community groups (Blouberg, Table View, Melkbosstrand, etc.).

Sign in
-------
What: Sign in with your approved Google account.
How: Click "Sign in with Google" on the first screen. Only allow-listed team emails
can use the app. If you see "Access not approved", your email is not on the list yet.


Generate a fresh batch
----------------------
What: Creates a new set of ads in one go.
How:
  1. Choose how many ads (5, 6, 8, or 10).
  2. Set the options below (photos, service, temperature, theme).
  3. Click "Generate ads" (or "Generate again" for a new batch).
While it runs, placeholder cards show until the results appear below.


Your photos
-----------
What: Add your own job photos so the AI writes copy that matches real work and can
use those images in ads.
How:
  1. Pick a photo file (JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF).
  2. Choose which service the photo belongs to (rug, carpet, upholstery, etc.).
  3. Click "Upload photo".
Uploaded photos are stored in your account and mixed into the next generation.
Photos you upload just before generating are prioritised to appear in that batch.


Uploads only (test)
-------------------
What: Test mode — every ad uses only your uploaded photos (no AI-made images).
How: Tick "Uploads only (test)". You need at least one upload. The batch size is
limited to how many photos you have (up to 10). "Focus on a service" is disabled;
the service comes from how each photo was tagged when uploaded.


Force fresh AI images
---------------------
What: When an ad needs an AI image, always create a brand-new one instead of reusing
a saved image from the cache.
How: Tick "Force fresh AI images". Uses more AI quota but gives new visuals. New
images are still saved for possible reuse later. Not available in uploads-only mode.


Focus on a service
------------------
What: All ads in the batch promote one cleaning service (e.g. only upholstery).
How: Tap a service chip: Loose rug, Fitted carpet, Upholstery, Mattress, Home,
Office, or Any (mix of services). When you pick one service, the tool uses matching
uploaded photos where possible and writes copy for that service only.


Copy temperature
----------------
What: Controls how formal, funny, or "campaign-like" the wording and AI image mood feel.
How:
  - Use presets: Humorous, Neutral, or Campaign manager, or drag the slider (0–100).
  - Low (0–33): witty, playful local tone; AI images slightly more upbeat.
  - Middle (34–66): warm neighbourly posts like your usual Facebook style (default).
  - High (67–100): polished ad-manager tone; more professional images.
Copy temperature affects AI-generated images only. Your own uploaded photos are unchanged.


Seasonal / holiday angle
------------------------
What: Steers the message toward a time of year or occasion.
How: Pick a theme chip, or leave "Auto" so the tool picks from the calendar
(upcoming holidays, current season in Cape Town). Examples: Summer, Christmas,
Mother's Day, Heritage Day, Back to school, Evergreen (no holiday angle).
Seasonal themes change the words, not outdoor/beach pictures — images stay on-brand
cleaning scenes indoors.


Understanding each generated ad
-------------------------------
Each card shows:
  - Service badge (which offering the ad is for).
  - Image: "Your photo", "Before & after", or "AI image".
  - "Fresh AI" or "Cached · used Nx" — whether the AI picture is new or reused
    from storage (saves cost and keeps consistency).
  - Occasion badge when the copy ties to a holiday or season.
  - Headline, body, call-to-action (with phone), and hashtags.

The AI is trained on your real past posts and brand notes so new ads sound like
West Coast Cleaners, not generic marketing — unless you set a high copy temperature.


Edit copy
---------
What: Fix wording before you post to Facebook.
How: Click "Edit copy" on a card. Change headline, body, CTA, or hashtags, then
Save. "Reset to original" undoes your edits back to what the AI first generated.
An "Edited" badge appears when you changed anything.

When you save changes, your improved wording can be stored in a copy library so
future batches learn from lines you liked (especially per service).


Download post
-------------
What: Builds one square PNG with your image, headline, body, CTA, and branding —
ready to upload as a single image post to a Facebook group.
How: Click "Download post" on a card (you need an image; finish editing first).
The file downloads to your device; upload it to the group like any other photo post.
You can still copy/paste the text separately if you prefer a text-only post.


Sign out
--------
What: Ends your session on this device.
How: Use "Sign out" at the top right.


Quick workflow (recommended)
----------------------------
1. Sign in.
2. Upload a few fresh job photos (tag the correct service).
3. Set copy temperature and seasonal theme if you want something specific.
4. Choose batch size and service focus, then Generate ads.
5. Edit any lines you want to tweak, then Download post for each one you will use.
6. Post to your Facebook groups.

For questions about access or technical setup, contact your developer / admin.
